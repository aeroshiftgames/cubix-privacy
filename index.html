Privacy Policy for Cubix: Block Master

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Cubix: Block Master ("Cubix", "the Game") is developed and published by AeroShift Games.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use Cubix, including information processed through advertising services, in-app purchases, and platform services.

2. Information We Collect

AeroShift Games does not require users to create an account to play Cubix. The Game does not directly request your name, home address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone access, or precise location.

Certain technical information may be processed automatically by third-party services used by the Game. Depending on the user's age, privacy choices, region, device, and applicable law, this information may include:

  • Device and operating system information
  • App version and basic technical diagnostics
  • Advertising interaction information
  • Approximate or general region information
  • Purchase and transaction status provided by Google Play
  • Information required for fraud prevention, security, and service delivery

3. Advertising

Cubix may display advertising using Unity Ads / Unity Monetization services.

The Game may contain:

  • Banner advertisements displayed in designated areas of the Game
  • Rewarded advertisements that users may voluntarily watch in exchange for an in-game benefit, such as continuing after the game board becomes full

Rewarded advertisements are optional and are only initiated when the user chooses to watch an advertisement.

Advertising behavior may vary depending on age, privacy status, region, consent status, and applicable legal requirements.

4. Children and Mixed-Age Audience

Cubix is intended for a general and mixed-age audience and may be played by both children and adults.

AeroShift Games takes additional steps to protect child users and users whose age is unknown.

Where required, Cubix and its advertising services are configured to limit advertising and data processing for children and users of unknown age. Personalized or interest-based advertising is not intended to be shown to users identified as children.

For child users and users of unknown age, advertising may be limited to contextual or non-personalized advertising as required by applicable law and platform policies.

Cubix does not intentionally ask children to provide personal information such as their real name, phone number, email address, exact location, or other direct identifiers in order to play the Game.

5. In-App Purchases

Cubix may offer optional in-app purchases, including consumable gameplay items or power-ups.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. AeroShift Games does not directly receive or store your full payment card information.

Google Play may process transaction information according to Google's own privacy and payment policies.

6. Local Game Data

Cubix may store certain gameplay information locally on your device, including:

  • Game settings
  • Language preference
  • High score
  • Unlocked cosmetic themes
  • Owned gameplay power-ups

This information is primarily used to preserve your progress and preferences.

7. Third-Party Services

Cubix may use third-party services to provide advertising, payments, platform functionality, security, analytics, or technical services.

These services may include:

  • Unity Ads / Unity Monetization
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing

Third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and may process data independently from AeroShift Games.

8. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Cubix may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the Game
  • Provide advertisements
  • Deliver rewarded advertisement benefits
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Maintain security and stability
  • Improve performance and compatibility
  • Comply with legal and platform requirements

9. Data Sharing

AeroShift Games does not sell personal information directly.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers when necessary to operate advertising, payments, security, platform functionality, or legal compliance.

10. Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data may remain on your device until the application data is cleared or the Game is uninstalled.

Third-party service providers may retain information according to their own legal obligations, privacy policies, and retention practices.

11. User Rights and Privacy Requests

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights related to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection regarding personal data.

You may contact AeroShift Games with privacy-related questions or requests.

12. Security

AeroShift Games takes reasonable measures to protect information and reduce unauthorized access, misuse, or loss.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Cubix, its advertising systems, purchasing systems, legal requirements, or third-party services change.
